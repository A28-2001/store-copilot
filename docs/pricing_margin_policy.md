## Category margin targets
Target gross margins by category: Produce 35%, Meat & Seafood 28%, Dairy & Eggs 32%, Pantry 38%, Snacks 40%, Beverages & Juices 45%, Cafe & Hot Bar 65%, Supplements 48%, Beauty & Body 55%. Category leads review actual margin against target every week.

## One price across stores and the app
Every SKU has one shelf price, the same in every store and in the app. A store-specific price needs written approval from the COO, and the approval is logged with an end date.

## Cost increases
When a vendor cost increase drops a SKU's margin more than 5 points below its category target, the category lead proposes a new shelf price within 3 days. The same price goes live in the stores and the app on the same day. Until the new price is approved, the SKU stays at the current price.

## Markdowns
Markdowns are capped at 30% off without approval. Anything deeper needs approval from the COO. The pre-approved perishable markdowns in the Perishables and Waste SOP count as approved.
