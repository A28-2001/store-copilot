*(illustrative) A synthetic checklist written for a portfolio demo. Not any real company's checklist.*

## Before the first sale
Every POS code at the new store is mapped to a master SKU before the first sale. Run test transactions on the top 100 SKUs and confirm each one lands on the right master SKU in reporting.

## Unmapped and duplicate codes
Any POS code that shows up in sales without a master SKU is fixed within 48 hours. Duplicate codes for the same product are merged into one, and the sales history is moved to the surviving code.

## Reading the numbers early
Treat a new store's trends as low confidence until day 30. Do not reset par levels, drop SKUs or change staffing on fewer than 30 days of the store's own history.

## Reorder quantities
Until the new store has 30 days of its own history, reorder quantities use the flagship's 14-day velocity scaled by the new store's size factor (for example 0.55 for a store about half the flagship's size).
