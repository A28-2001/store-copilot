*(illustrative) A synthetic procedure written for a portfolio demo. Not any real company's procedure.*

## Hot bar
Hot bar food is discarded after 4 hours on the line. Every pan gets a time label when it goes out, and every discard is logged by item and weight at the end of each shift.

## Cold-pressed juice
Cold-pressed juice bottles are pulled at the end of day 3 after delivery, whatever the printed date says. Pulled bottles are logged as a "day-3 juice pull" so the juice vendor credit can be claimed.

## Produce
Produce gets a 30% markdown one day before it reaches peak ripeness. Anything that is damaged or past peak is pulled and logged as waste the same day.

## Meat and seafood
Meat and seafood rotate first in, first out. Items get a 30% markdown on the day before their sell-by date and are pulled at close on the sell-by date.

## Waste target and review
The waste target is under 3% of perishable sales, measured at cost (waste cost divided by perishable cost of goods sold). If a store is over 3% for 2 weeks in a row, the store manager and operations run a waste review by category.
