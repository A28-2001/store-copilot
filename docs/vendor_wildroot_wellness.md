## Delivery
One delivery a week, on Wednesday, with a 7-day lead time.

## Minimum order and payment terms
The minimum order is $1,000 per store per delivery. Payment terms are Net 30 from the invoice date.

## Returns and credits
Unopened product can be returned within 60 days for credit, less a 15% restocking fee. The restocking fee is waived for any product that fails the Clean Ingredient Standard.

## Shelf life at delivery
Every supplement must arrive with at least 12 months of shelf life left. Product with less than 12 months left can be refused at the door.
