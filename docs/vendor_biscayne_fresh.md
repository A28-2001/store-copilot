## Delivery
Deliveries Monday to Saturday between 6 and 9 AM. Orders placed by 2 PM arrive the next morning, a 1-day lead time.

## Minimum order and payment terms
The minimum order is $300 per store per delivery. Payment terms are Net 15 from the invoice date.

## Returns and credits
Damaged or spoiled produce is credited in full if the store reports it with a photo within 24 hours of delivery. Credits appear on the next invoice.

## Seasonal substitution
Biscayne may substitute same-grade local produce when an item is out of season, if it tells the store by 5 AM on the delivery day. The store may refuse a substitution at the door.
