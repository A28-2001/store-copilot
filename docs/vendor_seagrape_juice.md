## Delivery
Deliveries every day before 7 AM with a 1-day lead time. Juice is pressed the day before delivery.

## Minimum order and payment terms
The minimum order is $250 per store per delivery. Payment terms are Net 15 from the invoice date.

## Returns and credits
Unsold bottles pulled at the end of day 3, as the Perishables and Waste SOP requires, are credited at 50% of cost, up to 10% of that week's order. Bottles delivered with fewer than 4 days of shelf life are credited in full.

## Exclusive flavors
Seagrape makes two rotating flavors only for us and does not sell them to other retailers within 3 miles of any of our stores.
