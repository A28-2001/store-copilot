## Banned ingredients
Food, drink and supplements may not contain seed oils (canola, sunflower, safflower, soybean, corn, cottonseed, grapeseed or rice bran oil), stevia, monk fruit, gums (guar, xanthan, gellan, carrageenan, locust bean), artificial colors, artificial sweeteners, artificial preservatives, or "natural flavors". Beauty and body products may not contain artificial fragrance or artificial colors.

## What each status means
Every SKU carries one status. Pass means it meets this standard. Review means a possible issue was found (a reformulation, an unclear label, a missing ingredient list); the item may stay on the shelf for up to 30 days while the brand sends a full ingredient declaration, and if it is not resolved in 30 days it becomes Fail. Fail means it contains a banned ingredient: it is pulled from every store and the app within 48 hours, and the vendor is notified in writing the same day.

## Who owns the standard
Brand partnerships owns this standard and the status of every SKU. Every new SKU is reviewed against the banned list before it gets a POS price. No review, no price, no sale. Store teams report suspected issues to brand partnerships instead of pulling items on their own.

## New local brands
New local and emerging brands get a 60-day trial. During the trial the SKU is tagged "trial" in the POS. At day 60, brand partnerships reviews sell-through, margin and clean status, then either adds the brand to the core range or ends the trial.
