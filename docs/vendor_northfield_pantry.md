## Delivery
One delivery a week, on Monday, with a 5-day lead time.

## Minimum order and payment terms
The minimum order is $1,500 per store per delivery. Payment terms are Net 45 from the invoice date.

## Returns and credits
Items damaged in transit are credited if the damage is noted on the delivery receipt. Items delivered with fewer than 90 days to their best-by date are credited in full.

## Price protection
Northfield gives 30 days' written notice before any cost increase. Orders placed during the notice window are billed at the old cost.
